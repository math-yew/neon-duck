Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 27, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Your name and email address, when you join a waitlist or contact us
  • Artwork images, overlay images/video, and any notes you submit through our forms
  • Basic usage information, such as which pages you visit and which link brought you to the site

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to waitlist signups and contact inquiries
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Understand which marketing links and channels are driving signups
  • Send you updates about StareBox, such as launch announcements or your promised discount code

3. Third-Party Services

We use StaticForms, a third-party form-processing service, to receive and route waitlist and contact form submissions to us by email. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Cookies & Tracking

We may use basic, privacy-respecting analytics to understand how visitors use the site. Links we share may include a tracking parameter in the URL (for example, `?ref=...`) so we can see which distributed link led to a given signup; this parameter is passed along with your form submission but is not used to identify you personally beyond that context.

5. Data Retention

We retain the information you submit for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry, operate your account, or as required by law.

6. Your Rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by contacting us.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
